Security & Compliance

Built for India. Secure by design.

KIBO360 HMS is ABDM-ready and aligned with the DPDP Act 2023, with clinical documentation that supports NABH accreditation - all on an encrypted, role-based, fully audited platform.

ABDM & ABHA Ready

Create and link ABHA health IDs and connect to India's Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission.

DPDP Act 2023 Aligned

Patient data handling aligned with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

NABH-Aligned Records

Clinical documentation and audit trails structured to meet NABH accreditation requirements.

Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Granular permissions ensure doctors, nurses, billers and admins see only what they need.

AES-256 Data Encryption

All clinical and financial data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).

Two-Factor Authentication

Optional 2FA for all administrative and privileged user accounts.

Audit Logs & Trail

Tamper-evident logs of every patient record access, edit, billing change and discharge.

Backups & Disaster Recovery

Automated multi-region backups with 99.9% uptime SLA and instant failover.
